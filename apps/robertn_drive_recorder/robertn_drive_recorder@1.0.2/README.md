# Drive recorder

Keeps the car's cameras recording and saves a clip when you tap **Save clip** (by default the 20 s before and the 10 s
after). **Clips** lists them newest first; open one to watch it (only while the car stands still), switch cameras,
lock it, delete it or copy it to a USB stick (as recorded, or one camera as an MP4). **Settings** has the clip length,
Start with the car and the storage used (over 4 GB the oldest unlocked clips are replaced).

The Store installs IterioDev's **Drive Recorder player** (`iteriodev-drplay`) with it.
